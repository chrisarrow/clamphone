How it works

  • Opening. On a foldable, unfold the phone and the clam opens with it. On other phones, tap the clam.
  • Growing. A pearl grows while the clam stays shut. Open it too soon and you get sand. Give it a minute for a pearl, a night for the rare ones. The easy way: shut it at bedtime, open it at breakfast.
  • Peeking. Crack the phone open a little and look through the gap. The light inside shows how it’s doing: silver while it’s young, gold when it’s ready.
  • Gently. Open it slowly. Rush it and the clam flinches. Keep rushing it and it swims off to hide, until you close your phone. Its pearl keeps growing either way.
  • Visitors. Leave the clam open and empty and now and then something wanders in: a sprat, a shrimp, a pufferfish, a coin someone wished on. Close it gently and it’s yours. Rush it and it’s gone.
  • The sea floor. Close the clam on a find and it coughs it out onto the sand, and the next pearl starts growing. You can also drag a find out yourself and set it down wherever you like. The sand holds seven finds; after that the oldest goes into the sea chest. Tap the chest, then tap a find to take it out.
  • The necklace. String your pearls on silk, in whatever order you like. Sixteen make a necklace, and it lies in a loop on the sand among your finds.
  • The journal. The menu is a sea chart. It keeps a ledger of all 40 finds, your numbers, the dress-ups you have earned, and your achievements.

Questions

I only got sand.
It was opened too soon. Shut it and give it a minute.
It flinched and swam off.
It was opened too fast. Close your phone and it comes back. Its pearl kept growing while it was gone.
How do I catch a visitor?
Leave the clam open with nothing inside and wait. When something wanders in, close the phone slowly. Rush it and the visitor is gone.
How do I know it’s ready without opening it?
Peek through the crack: gold light means ready. When it is ready, the bubbles the shut clam lets out catch a little gold too. The widgets and the Lock Screen countdown show it as well.
How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Clamphone. Open the app once first so the widget can show your own clam.
Can I see the pearl growing on the Lock Screen?
Yes. Turn on “Pearl on the Lock Screen” in the menu’s settings, and a countdown shows until it is ready.
Can it tell me when a pearl is ready?
Yes. Turn on “Tell me when it’s ready” in the menu’s settings. The notification is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
The light moves when I tilt the phone.
It is meant to. Turn off “Light follows tilt” in the settings to keep it still. It is also still when Reduce Motion is on.
Does it drain the battery or warm the phone?
It shouldn’t. When nothing is happening the clam goes still and the app stops drawing. If your phone is warm or in Low Power Mode, it slows its animation down on its own.
Can I move my collection to a new phone?
Your clam lives only on your phone and is not synced. It moves with a full device backup or transfer, and is lost if the app is deleted.
