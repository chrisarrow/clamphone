CLAMPHONE: FOLD TO OPEN
Fact sheet

Developer: Arrow IT (Chris Arrow, solo developer)
Release: 23 October 2026, the day Apple's first foldable iPhone reaches customers. Out now on the App Store.
Price: Free. One optional one-time unlock, Pearl Diver (a lemon wedge, a captain's cap and six real scallop shells). No ads, no subscriptions. Free since version 1.1; earlier buyers get Pearl Diver included.
Platform: iPhone, iOS 27 or later. Made for the foldable; on a phone that doesn't fold, tap to open.
Privacy: No data collected. No accounts. Only Apple's App Store purchase system is used, for the optional unlock.
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clamphone-fold-to-open/id6820744662
Press kit: https://chrisarrow.github.io/clamphone/press.html
Contact: clam@chrisarrow.com

ONE PARAGRAPH
Apple's first foldable iPhone opens like a clam, so Clamphone turns it into one. A scallop lies on the sea floor, seen from straight above. Unfold the phone and the clam opens with the hinge, creaking all the way. Keep it shut and a pearl grows inside. Open it and you might find one of 21 pearls, or a rubber duck. Leave it open and something might wander in; close it gently and it's yours. It is a free novelty in the tradition of the old iBeer gag, where the phone becomes the object, played completely straight. No ads and no data collected; one optional unlock adds a wedge of lemon, for the clam that has everything.

SHORT
Your phone opens like a clam, so here is a clam. Keep it shut and a pearl grows. Unfold it gently to see. It might be a pearl. It might be a duck. Leave it open and something might wander in.

FEATURES
- The hinge is the controller: unfold the phone and the clam opens with it
- A real recorded creak
- Pearls grow while the clam stays shut; the longer, the rarer (open too soon and it's sand; a minute for a pearl, a night for the rare ones)
- 40 finds: 21 kinds of pearl, plus things that should not be in a clam
- Visitors wander into the open clam (a sprat, a shrimp, a pufferfish, a lucky coin); close it gently to keep one
- Passers-by: a crab, a shoal of fish, a drifting jellyfish
- String pearls on silk; sixteen make a necklace that lies on the sand
- Peek through the crack: the light inside shows how it's doing
- Open it gently: rush it and it flinches, then swims off until you close your phone
- Finds land on the sand; a sea chest rises when it's full
- A sea-chart journal with a ledger of every find, and achievements
- Dress-ups (limpet, barnacles, bow tie, coral tiara, top hat), earned by playing, plus an optional Pearl Diver unlock led by a wedge of lemon
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity
- A shareable specimen card, or a five-second clip with sound
